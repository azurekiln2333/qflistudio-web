## Round 3 — CSS grammar + real demo font

Two defects p6's browser probe found that the earlier SVG-decoding check
missed:

1. **Unquoted `url(data:image/svg+xml,…)` with literal spaces** — CSS parsing
   drops the declaration. Fixed in **css-062 / css-078 / css-088** (and their
   peers) by quoting the URL: `url("data:…")`. New guard
   `check_css_grammar.py` fails any unquoted `url()` containing
   whitespace/`"`/`'`/`(`/`)`.
2. **css-073 (`my.woff2`)** — a bare local font (404), then a 189-byte `AAAA…`
   fake header (broken placeholder). Replaced with a **real, original 576-byte
   WOFF2** generated by `scripts/curriculum/make_demo_font.py`
   (`FontBuilder`+`TTGlyphPen`, glyphs `W e b F o r g`, no vendor font).
   Reproducible: pinned `head.created/modified` + `recalcTimestamp=False`;
   two runs are byte-identical (SHA-256 verified).
